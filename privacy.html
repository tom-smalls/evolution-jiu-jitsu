Privacy Policy

How Evolution Gracie Jiu-Jitsu collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Evolution Gracie Jiu-Jitsu ("we", "us", "our") operates from 65 E Parade, Ilkley, LS29 8JP. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you enquire about, book, or attend our classes, and how we use and protect it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us by phone, email, or through our website — such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Booking & waiver information submitted through our third-party booking partner, CleverWaiver, when you register for a free trial or class, including emergency contact details where requested.
  • Attendance records such as which classes and programs you or your child take part in, and progress or grading history.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, if our website is hosted with analytics enabled.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and process class or trial bookings.
  • To manage attendance, safety and emergency contact records for students, particularly children.
  • To send you information about your classes, timetable changes, or membership.
  • To meet our legal, insurance and safeguarding obligations as a training centre working with children.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information with CleverWaiver, our booking and digital waiver provider, solely to process your registration. We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect the safety of a student.

Children's information

Where a child takes part in our Gracie Bullyproof® programs, we collect the information provided by a parent or guardian at sign-up, and that parent or guardian retains the right to review, update or request deletion of their child's records at any time.

How long we keep your data

We retain contact and attendance records for as long as you remain an active student, and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet insurance, safeguarding and legal record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you or your child, or ask us to restrict how we use it. To make a request, contact Francis directly at Evolutionjj@outlook.com or 07495 494979.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies required for the site to function correctly. We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent directly to Francis Robinson at Evolutionjj@outlook.com or by calling 07495 494979.
