A certified training centre, built on leverage — not strength

Our LineageEvery technique traces back to Helio Gracie, who adapted traditional Judo into a system built for a smaller, weaker person to prevail — the foundation Gracie University still teaches today.

Evolution Gracie Jiu-Jitsu is officially authorised under Gracie University in California, one of the world's most respected names in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. As the leading martial arts club in Ilkley for Gracie Jiu-Jitsu, every class runs in a friendly, safe environment designed to help students of every level pick up the full curriculum through clear, methodical instruction.

Gracie Jiu-Jitsu is built to let a smaller, weaker person control a bigger, stronger attacker — tiring them out and neutralising the threat without striking.

Rather than trading blows, students learn joint locks, chokes and control positions that let them make an aggressor work hard, make mistakes, and eventually give the student the opening they need — either to escape safely or to finish the encounter on their own terms.

Kids Martial Arts Club Ilkley · Ages 3–14 · No strikes, ever

We teach children verbal assertiveness to deter bullies, plus non-violent self-defence they can use if they're physically assaulted. There's no punching or kicking here — instead kids learn leverage-based control holds that neutralise a threat without turning them into an aggressor.

The programme runs on a five-point Rules of Engagement that every student rehearses in class:

  • 01 Avoid the fight at all costs.
  • 02 If physically attacked, defend yourself.
  • 03 If verbally attacked, talk, tell, tackle.
  • 04 Never punch or kick — establish control and negotiate.
  • 05 When applying submissions, use minimal force and negotiate.

Adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Ilkley · Beginner program · no experience required

Gracie Jiu-Jitsu spans hundreds of techniques, but Gracie Family research into real fights found that 36 of them account for the vast majority of successful outcomes. Combatives is entirely dedicated to mastering those 36 — one standing and one ground technique per hour-long lesson, broken into simple, repeatable drills.

There's no sparring in this program and no prerequisites — every class welcomes complete beginners, regardless of age, gender or fitness level.

Self-defence workshops for women · no experience needed

Women Empowered® is Gracie University's dedicated self-defence curriculum for women, focused on the situations women are statistically most likely to face — grabs, holds and attacks from a larger, stronger assailant, including from a standing clinch or on the ground.

Sessions are taught in a supportive, women-only environment and build the same leverage-based control used throughout Gracie Jiu-Jitsu, adapted specifically to close the size and strength gap against a bigger attacker.

  • Practical answers to realistic grabs, holds and ground attacks
  • Small-group, women-only training environment
  • No fitness or martial arts background required
  • Techniques scale to any size or strength difference
